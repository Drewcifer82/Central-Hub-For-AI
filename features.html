AI is evolving faster than any technology in history — and it's going to affect every person on the planet. The practice of deliberately building and optimizing your AI toolkit — knowing what each tool does, what it costs, where tools overlap, and where your coverage ends. Not collecting tools randomly. Not paying for subscriptions just to evaluate them. Building with intention.

Every day, new AI tools launch. Subscriptions stack up. Features overlap between tools you're already paying for. FAQ

Common questions

What is AI Stacking?

AI Stacking is the practice of deliberately building and optimizing the AI tools that power your workflow. It means knowing what each tool does, what it costs, where tools overlap, and where you have gaps — and making intentional decisions based on that. Central Hub For AI is the free workspace built specifically for this practice.

Is Central Hub For AI free?

Yes, completely free. There is no pricing plan, no free trial, and no premium tier. Every feature is available to everyone with no account required — and that is not changing.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no sign-up, no email address required. Your stack and settings are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is ever sent to any server.

What is an AI tool stack?

An AI tool stack is the collection of AI-powered software you use regularly in your work or creative process. Just like a developer has a tech stack, a modern professional has an AI stack — the set of tools that powers their workflow. Central Hub For AI helps you build, track, and optimize yours.

How does the Overlap Detector work?

The Overlap Detector compares the capability tags of every tool in your stack and surfaces pairs or groups of tools that share significant functionality. It flags potential redundancy so you can decide whether the overlap is intentional or an opportunity to consolidate and reduce your monthly AI spend.

How many AI tools are in the catalog?

The catalog currently includes 40+ AI tools spanning writing, image generation, video, audio, coding, research, and productivity categories. It is updated regularly as new tools gain traction and established tools add major features.
